# AI Tools Usage Disclosure

During the development of this project, I used artificial intelligence tools and cloud-based services as additional resources to support the development process. The main AI tool used was Anthropic Claude.

AI assistance was used at different stages of the project, primarily for programming support, problem-solving, code improvement, and debugging.

The following activities involved AI assistance:

- **Code Development:** AI tools were used to help write certain parts of the source code, suggest possible implementations, and assist with repetitive programming tasks.
- **Code Explanation:** AI was used to explain programming concepts, functions, libraries, and technical approaches that were relevant to the project.
- **Debugging and Error Handling:** During development, AI assistance was used to identify potential errors, understand compiler messages, troubleshoot unexpected behaviour, and suggest possible solutions.
- **Code Improvement:** AI tools helped review existing code, suggest improvements to readability, structure, naming conventions, and overall maintainability.
- **Problem-Solving:** When encountering technical difficulties, I used AI to explore different approaches, compare possible solutions, and understand the advantages and disadvantages of particular implementations.
- **Documentation and Research:** AI was occasionally used to clarify technical documentation and provide additional explanations of tools, technologies, and programming methods.

Cloud-based AI services were accessed to support these activities. In particular, Anthropic Claude was used as a programming assistant throughout parts of the development process.

It is important to clarify that AI tools were used as supporting resources rather than as a replacement for the development process. I used the generated suggestions as a reference, reviewed the proposed solutions, made modifications where necessary, and integrated relevant parts into the project.

The final project was reviewed and adjusted to ensure that the implementation followed the required specifications and project objectives.

The purpose of using these tools was to improve development efficiency, overcome technical challenges, gain a better understanding of the technologies involved, and support the overall quality of the final implementation.
